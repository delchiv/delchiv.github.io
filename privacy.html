Privacy policy

rclone-netops is used only by its owner, to copy the owner's backups to the owner's own Google Drive with rclone.

  • Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to upload, list and restore these backups.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared with any third party.
  • Access tokens are kept only on the owner's own servers.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
